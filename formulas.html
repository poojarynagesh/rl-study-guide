Gradient ascent on $J$
$\boldsymbol\theta_{t+1}=\boldsymbol\theta_t+\alpha\,\widehat{\nabla J(\boldsymbol\theta_t)}$, $\ \E[\widehat{\nabla J}]\approx\nabla J$

Performance (episodic)
$J(\boldsymbol\theta)\doteq v_{\pi_{\boldsymbol\theta}}(s_0)$

Policy gradient theorem
$\nabla J(\boldsymbol\theta)\propto\sum_s\mu(s)\sum_aq_\pi(s,a)\nabla\pi(a|s,\boldsymbol\theta)$ (no $\nabla\mu$, no $\nabla q$)

Expectation form
$\nabla J\propto\E_\pi\Big[\sum_aq_\pi(S_t,a)\nabla\pi(a|S_t,\boldsymbol\theta)\Big]=\E_\pi\Big[G_t\dfrac{\nabla\pi(A_t|S_t,\boldsymbol\theta)}{\pi(A_t|S_t,\boldsymbol\theta)}\Big]$

$\mathbf w_{t+1}=\mathbf w_t+\alpha\big[R_{t+1}+\gamma\max_a\hat q(S_{t+1},a,\mathbf w_t)-\hat q(S_t,A_t,\mathbf w_t)\big]\nabla\hat q(S_t,A_t,\mathbf w_t)$
16.3
Bellman optimality ($Q^*$)
$Q^*(s,a)=\E_{s'}\big[r+\gamma\max_{a'}Q^*(s',a')\mid s,a\big]$
lecture Nov 2
DQN target
$y=\begin{cases}r&\text{terminal }s'\\ r+\gamma\max_{a'}Q(s',a';\theta^-)&\text{otherwise}\end{cases}$
paper
Loss
$L_i(\theta_i)=\E_{(s,a,r,s')\sim U(D)}\Big[\big(r+\gamma\max_{a'}Q(s',a';\theta_i^-)-Q(s,a;\theta_i)\big)^2\Big]$
paper
Gradient ($\theta^-$ fixed)
$\nabla_{\theta_i}L_i=-2\,\E\Big[\big(y-Q(s,a;\theta_i)\big)\nabla_{\theta_i}Q(s,a;\theta_i)\Big]$
paper
SGD step on one tuple
$\theta_{i+1}=\theta_i+\alpha\big[y-Q(s,a;\theta_i)\big]\nabla_{\theta_i}Q(s,a;\theta_i)$, error $y-Q$ clipped to $[-1,1]$
§16.5
Target-network update
every $C$ updates $\theta^-\leftarrow\theta$: $\ \theta_i^-=\theta_{C\lfloor i/C\rfloor}$ (lag $0..C-1$)
§16.5
Reward clipping

§16.5.2
Uniform replay
$\Pr(\text{tuple})=1/T$ for $T$ stored tuples; buffer keeps the last $N$
